The record.

The pattern in every engagement: AI adoption dies where the tool doesn’t live. So I build inside the tools that are already open.

For Wheel the World, an accessible-travel startup, I built an AI image system that made 100GB of unsearchable assets findable in seconds. It passed 100,000 API uses in its first month. Their marketing team also got AI-driven CRM automation inside HubSpot: response rates doubled, and the team adopted it inside two weeks.

For Larta Institute, a federal research accelerator running programs for NOAA and USDA, I designed a hybrid retrieval architecture for domain-specific documents and delivered the production spec, with cost modeling, that their enterprise AI pilot adopted.

For ShoplyAI, I build content infrastructure: an AI-assisted production pipeline with hard constraints against generic machine writing.

And I run TinyMacro, where the same discipline became a product: one AI employee per company, doing a real job end to end. My consulting and my company feed each other; you get what I’ve proven on my own operation.
