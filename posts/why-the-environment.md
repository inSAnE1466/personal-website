I had not stopped to ask why - my life's purpose is not to build something that will make other people money. It is not to sell business insurance, I am not selling speed. I have no proclivity for enterprise adoption as a conceptual business model in and of itself. I do not care about money, or maximizing profit. I see a clear path forward - formal growth strategies based on what is working, picking markets that larger FDE corporations are not working on, and provisioning a system that lets me build boilerplate apps - built and on retainer. I see myself hiring people my age to perform the tasks that I don't want to do - or to scale using the same inputs that I already have created. I see myself consistently more AI-native - I already conduct basically my entire business administration, accounting, and CRM through a custom harness, CLIs, and documentation in a singular monorepo.

I see myself traveling, exploring the world, learning hard lessons about the business, it is within reach - I have no rush, I am relishing in this process - how fascinating!

But none of this is EMPOWERED by a greater impact. I am an environmentalist at heart. There is little that I find intrinsic to myself as a human, that was not embroidered into my soul by external forces. But I have loved nature my entire life, and along with great strides in biotech, ML, and robotics (and AI engineering), important, and alarming, problems must be solved in the future - like the promulgation of environmentally sustainable practices.

It is no secret that enterprise pollutes a lot more than consumer. Which is why I find myself, in the intersection of these two passions, wanting to build in this industry. I want to establish everything above as a business owner, but I will never feel a call to action unless it is for something that I care about as deeply as the environment.
