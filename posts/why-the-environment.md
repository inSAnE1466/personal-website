title: Why I want this pointed at the environment
description: Building an AI business is not enough of a reason on its own.
draft: true
---
For the last several years I have been fascinated by entrepreneurial adventures. Building a business is such a fascinating strategy game to me. A brutal, compelling version of it comes with owning your own business: so many things to tend to, different arguments and points of view, decisions to make, and restricted variables (time, spend, people, network).

I have enjoyed learning so much over the last several years in tech, and over the last few months with my own business, that I forgot to assess my greater purpose: the impact I want to make, and the industry I want to make it in.

I think AI is the most compelling innovation of my lifetime so far. I very much enjoy the challenge of putting new technology to work on the product side. But everyone is building the same thing: superapps, connected intravenously to the greater systems of a company, building workflows to save time, money and effort.

I had not stopped to ask why. My life's purpose is not to build something that will make other people money. It is not to sell business insurance. I am not selling speed. I have no proclivity for enterprise adoption as a business model in and of itself, and I do not care about maximizing profit.

I see a clear path forward: growth strategies based on what is working, markets that larger firms are not working on, and a system that lets me build custom software and keep it running for people. I already run basically my entire business administration, accounting and CRM through a custom harness, CLIs and documentation in a single repository. I see myself traveling, exploring the world, and learning hard lessons about the business. It is within reach. I have no rush. I am relishing this process. How fascinating!

But none of this is empowered by a greater impact. I am an environmentalist at heart. There is little I find intrinsic to myself as a person that was not embroidered into my soul by something outside me. But I have loved nature my entire life. Alongside great strides in biotech, machine learning, robotics and AI engineering, important and alarming problems still have to be solved, like getting sustainable practices adopted.

I believe enterprise pollutes a lot more than consumers do. That is why, at the intersection of these two passions, I want to build here. I want to establish everything above as a business owner, but I will never feel a call to action unless it is for something I care about as deeply as the environment.
